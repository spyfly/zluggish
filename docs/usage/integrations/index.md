# Integrations

Connect Zluggish to the services you use to upload activities, sync planned workouts and share your rides.

- [Strava](./strava.md) — upload activities with the Strava API or a browser session.
- [Garmin Connect](./garmin.md) — upload activities to your Garmin Connect account.
- [Intervals.icu and planned workouts](./intervals-icu.md) — sync planned workouts from Intervals.icu or import TrainingPeaks exports.
- [Official Zwift uploads and Runalyze](./zwift-and-runalyze.md) — upload to your official Zwift account and use an existing Runalyze token.
